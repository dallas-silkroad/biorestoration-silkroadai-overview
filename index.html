The pattern we see

The busier a practice gets, the more leaks out the back

A practice with a dozen providers and thousands of patients does not stall for lack of demand. It hits a ceiling at the front desk and in the back office, where every new patient adds phone calls, scheduling conflicts, follow-ups, and reconciliation that nobody has time for. Here are the bottlenecks we see most often in practices at your scale.

Silk Road AI SolutionsOperations & Growth
Bottlenecks, part one · the front of house
📅

Double-booked, shuffled appointments

Multi-provider schedules with shared rooms and equipment collide. Every moved or doubled appointment is lost revenue and an irritated patient.

📞

A front desk buried in the phone

Coordinators spend the day answering calls with no fast pathway for the caller, so questions pile up and bookings quietly slip away.

🧾

Packages reconciled by hand

Memberships, injectable packages, shots, and supplements get tracked on spreadsheets. It is slow, easy to get wrong, and a daily time sink.

🧪

Lab and order confusion

Patients arrive for labs or treatments unsure what was ordered, and staff drop what they are doing to chase it down.

Silk Road AI SolutionsOperations & Growth
Bottlenecks, part two · growth and margin
📥

Leads that never get a second touch

Inquiries get worked through personal phones and memory. Good prospects go cold because follow-up depends on someone remembering.

🔗

Systems that do not talk

Scheduling, records, inventory, and marketing each live in their own tool, so the team re-keys the same information all day long.

📦

Inventory and product waste

Without tight tracking, expired and over-ordered product quietly writes off thousands of dollars a year.

⚠

Marketing that trips the rules

Google and Meta restrict ads for PRP, hormones, and GLP-1 medications, so campaigns get thin or disapproved without a compliant plan.

What this could look like here

Same ceiling, bigger practice

Biorestoration is one of Utah's largest hormone and aesthetics practices, with roughly 14 providers, a 4.7-star reputation across hundreds of reviews, and growing GLP-1 and regenerative demand. That scale is a strength, and it is exactly where the ceiling shows up: more providers and more patients mean more scheduling conflicts, more follow-ups, and more to reconcile.

A sensible first step
  • Map the current tools you use for scheduling, records, inventory, and marketing.
  • Find the one or two bottlenecks costing the most in time and money today.
  • Fix those first, prove the number, then decide what is worth doing next.
